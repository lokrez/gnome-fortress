## Native language

32×40 RGBA characters, 32×32 opaque terrain. Top-down tiles retain their exact existing coordinates; characters face the observer at a slight angle. Dark peat outlines are one native pixel thick. Highlights come from the upper left. Nearest-neighbor scaling only. Character height remains 90% of the displayed tile size; inspection portraits provide the close detail. At map scale, silhouettes matter more than stitching.

Eight hat profiles: hooked tower, folded bonnet, sagging patchwork, moss crown, mushroom brim, forward hook, slender spire, crooked broad brim. These form one family of small bearded garden creatures. Large hats, hidden eyes, protruding noses, ragged beards, tiny coats and boots establish identity. No equipment implies unsupported professions. Identity selects silhouette, restrained cloth colors and beard decoration; these are cosmetics, not inherited traits.

## World and archive

Vegetation is irregular and restrained. Forest has twisted trunks and clustered foliage, stone has broken facets and lichen, water is dark teal, hazards are exposed dark fissures rather than fire. Deterministic neighborhood edge stippling softens tile boundaries without changing walkability. Decoration is not a resource or simulated organism.

Historical treatments use actual last recorded transitions only: rupture scar stitches, rewild growth over fracture marks, wellspring mineral rim. Repeated transitions remain in the Land record. H disables these treatments and their badges. Rain and Drought remain thin, distinct active-region marks; neither implies changed water terrain.

The UI is a naturalist's field journal: warm paper, aged ink, double ruled margins, copper tabs and restrained botanical corner marks. Armed powers use a dark rust fill and explicit existing mode text. Decorative motifs do not obscure hit regions. Death portraits are archival skull marks, never living map agents.

## Reproducible assets

`gnome_fortress/pixel_art.py` is the original asset source and replaceable API. All native surfaces and scaled surfaces are bounded LRU caches. No runtime generation service, external artwork, or new dependency. `research/g8a1_evidence.py` exports the actual assets and running-build captures. Artists can replace native-returning functions with same-sized PNG loads without touching the simulation or camera.

Owner approval of the implementation remains pending. A coherent procedural pixel language is the deliverable; it does not promise the painted complexity of the larger concept sheet.

## Asset API and cache budget

- `appearance(id)` returns `(id % 8, (id * 7 + 3) % 4, (id * 11 + 1) % 3)`: hat silhouette, coat, beard tone. Stable mathematical cosmetics, independent of world seed and RNG.
- `gnome_native(style, sleeping, phase)`: 32×40 awake, 40×32 reclining. Two recorded-walk foot poses. No invented idle activities or unpaused sleep walking. `gnome_sprite` scales relative to native 40px height and returns the exact displayed alpha mask; surface shadows below mask threshold are not clickable bodies.
- `terrain_native(kind, variant)`: 32×32, eight position-hashed variants used by the viewer. Native coordinate grain uses a fixed integer hash, not an RNG. `terrain_decoration` normalizes neighbors into four different-terrain boundary bits. The latest actual terrain transition supplies the history key.
- `illustrated_tile`: opaque, precomposed terrain/edge/history art. Food bars, active regions, queued requests and selection remain separate live overlays. No changing biological data is baked into art caches.
- `death_portrait`, `icon`, `journal_panel`: archival skull, five power and objective-record glyphs, and original paper grain/botanical margins. No additional factual strings are generated by these assets.
- LRU capacities: native gnomes 256; scaled gnomes/masks 512; native terrain 128; scaled terrain 512; composed terrain 1024; edge/history layers 1024; glyphs 128; panel sizes 64; archival portrait sizes 32. Caches are disposable and never serialized. The camera's native display tile ceiling remains 96px.
- Sprite height remains 0.9× tile size, with existing deterministic crowd offsets, nine displayed occupants per dense tile, selected-last priority and cycling of all occupants. There are no new world positions or interpolated movement.
- Typography uses the bundled Pygame font at native screen resolution. Text is not bitmap-scaled with the art. Terrain transitions use the word “to” because the bundled font did not render the old arrow consistently.

Asset replacement boundary: replace the native generators with locally loaded original PNGs of the same dimensions and alpha conventions; preserve the function signatures, style IDs and scaling/mask contract. The simulation, checkpoint format and camera need no changes.
