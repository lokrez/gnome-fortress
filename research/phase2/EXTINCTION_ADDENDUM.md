# Descriptive extinction analysis addendum

Recorded after the first completed ablated world was observed extinct, before all
20 pairs completed. The pre-specified lifespan and hazard endpoints, seed set,
horizon, paired bootstrap and treatment are unchanged.

Add a descriptive summary of world extinctions by condition, first extinction tick
from the final death record, and all population trajectories. For within-condition
extinction fractions show exact binomial 95% reference intervals across 20 seeds.
This avoids portraying a degenerate resampling interval from 0/20 or 20/20 as proof
of zero uncertainty. These intervals are NOT paired risk-difference intervals and
are an exploratory supplement, not a replacement for the primary paired analyses.
They assume independent seeded trials under this model, not real biological worlds.
No new simulations or treatment changes are introduced.
